Business Performance Dashboard for a Retail Company

Objective:

This project mirrors the most common task found in the job descriptions: creating an end-to-end business intelligence dashboard to track performance and provide actionable insights for stakeholders.


How it Reflects the Job Descriptions:

It directly addresses the need to "design and develop Power BI reports and dashboards that effectively convey insights".

You will "write complex SQL queries to extract data from multiple sources" and "transform it into meaningful insights".

The outcome is a high-level interactive dashboard that allows management teams to "easily grasp progress and guide strategy".

Tools and Skills You Will Use:

SQL: To clean, aggregate, and join data from different tables (e.g., sales, customers, products).

Power BI or Tableau: To create data models and build interactive visualizations.

Excel: To perform initial data exploration or validation.

Data Storytelling: To structure your dashboard in a way that tells a clear and compelling story.

Business Acumen: To define relevant Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) like sales growth, customer acquisition cost, and profit margins.

Step-by-Step Guide:

Find a Dataset: Use a publicly available dataset. The "Superstore Sales Dataset" (available on Kaggle) is an excellent choice as it contains sales, customer, and product information.

Set Up Your Database: Load the dataset (which is often in CSV format) into a local database like PostgreSQL or MySQL. This allows you to practice writing SQL queries in a realistic environment.

Define Business Questions: Like the following,
What are our sales and profit trends over time?
Which product categories are the most profitable?
Who are our most valuable customers?
How does performance vary by region?

Data Extraction and Transformation (SQL): Write SQL queries to create the tables you'll need for your dashboard.
This involves:

Joining sales tables with customer and product tables.
Creating calculated columns (e.g., Profit Margin = (Profit / Sales)).
Grouping data by date, region, and category to pre-aggregate results.

Build the Dashboard (Power BI/Tableau): Connect your BI tool to the database. Create a dashboard with multiple pages (e.g., Sales Overview, Product Analysis, Customer Insights). Use a variety of charts (bar charts, line graphs, maps, tables) to visualize your KPIs.
Add interactive filters and slicers so users can drill down into the data (e.g., filter by year, region).


Summarize Your Findings: On the main page of your dashboard, add a text box with 3-4 bullet points summarizing the key takeaways and providing data-driven recommendations
